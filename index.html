About Janice Rosen Inc.

Good food trade runs on relationships. For more than fifteen years, building them has been my work.

Janice Rosen Inc. (JRI) specializes in international food trade, sourcing, importing, exporting and business development for retail and foodservice markets.

We connect Canadian and international manufacturers, processors, importers and distributors with new opportunities across Canada and around the world, across fresh, frozen and shelf-stable foods.

Every partnership gets direct, personal attention. When you work with JRI, you work with me.

Import & export

Retail & foodservice market development
